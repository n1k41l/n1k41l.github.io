Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

"Hill Farmhouse Home Assistant" is a personal integration used by a single household to connect its own Google Nest thermostat to its own self-hosted Home Assistant server.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's permission, the integration reads thermostat state (temperature, humidity, mode and set point) and sends thermostat commands through Google's Smart Device Management API.

Where it goes

The data is stored only on the household's own Home Assistant server. It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or any purpose other than controlling the household's own heating.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page.
